About me

I'm a backend developer and AI engineer based in Cairo, working across the stack behind the interface — APIs, databases, queues, and the AI systems that power modern products.

Over the past two and a half years I've built systems for management, e-commerce and internal tooling projects: services that solve practical problems, handle real data, and are built around the needs of the people using them. Lately, I've been focusing more on SaaS products, especially CRM and business management systems, and I've built several projects in that space.

I've always had a strong interest in databases and backend architecture. Even in college, I cared more about building complete projects around what I was learning than simply finishing assignments. I care about typed code, honest error handling, and infrastructure that's boring in the best way — predictable, observable, and easy to hand off to the next engineer.
